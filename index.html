Three Core Economic Superpowers:

  • 70% Product Margins: Raw materials (casting plaster, acrylics, mini brushes, and boxes) cost approximately $5.50 per child. Selling at $18 to $20 leaves ~$13 gross margin per kit.
  • Batch Fulfillment Schedule: Because parents order by party date, you can pack and batch all boxes on Tuesday nights for Friday/Saturday parties. No daily rushed fulfillment.
  • Built-In Viral Word of Mouth: Every party attended by 8 to 12 kids puts your branded box into the hands of 8 to 12 local parents whose own children will have birthdays later in the year!
