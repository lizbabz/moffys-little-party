# Moffy's Little Party

Boutique DIY Kids Party Kits direct-to-consumer e-commerce storefront.

## Key Features Built for Moffy

- **Bespoke Boutique Aesthetic**: Styled with a warm French/Scandinavian children's atelier aesthetic (Fraunces serif + Plus Jakarta Sans, warm ecru, soft clay, terracotta, and deep forest tones).
- **Zero AI Clichés**: No emojis and no dot-separated word phrases anywhere on the site.
- **Strict 6-Kit Minimum Rule**: Enforced across hero calculators, catalog cards, quick-view modal, and cart drawer steppers to guarantee high Average Order Values (AOV $120 to $350+).
- **Curated Collections for Boys & Explorers**: Dedicated action kits including the *Mini Monster Truck & Race Car Craft* (with rolling axles and racing decals), *Jurassic Dino Mold & Paint Kit*, and *Galaxy Glow Cosmic Slime Kit*.
- **Interactive Parent Savings & Business Blueprint**:
  - **Parent View**: Real-time calculator comparing typical entertainer fees ($450+) against Moffy's kits ($144 to $190), highlighting savings of over $270.
  - **Moffy's View (Business Model)**: Interactive unit economics calculator showing monthly party volume, 70% gross margins, and projected revenue ($2,850/mo part-time scaling to $79,800/yr).
- **Frictionless Parent Checkout**:
  - Slide-out cart drawer with free shipping progress bar.
  - 3-step checkout modal: Party Details (Child name, turning age, party date for personalized seals), Parent Shipping Contact, and Express Payment Simulation.
  - Printable order confirmation and 60-minute adult host cheat-sheet.

## How to View Locally

Simply double-click `index.html` to open it in any web browser, or run Python's built-in local server:

```bash
cd "/Users/oyin/Desktop/Website Projects/Moffy's Little Party"
python3 -m http.server 3000
```

Then visit [http://localhost:3000](http://localhost:3000) in your browser.
